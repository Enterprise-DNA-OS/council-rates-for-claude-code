# Why this target

Checked 19 November 2026.

- **The bill.** Snowy Valleys Council (NSW) records in its contracts register an agreement with Technology One Limited for "Annual SaaS Fees for OneCouncil software": A$141,023 in year 1 (discounted), then A$317,302 in year 2 and each later year, subject to CPI. That covers the whole OneCouncil suite, of which property and rating is one part. https://www.snowyvalleys.nsw.gov.au/files/assets/public/v/1/gipa/contracts-register/q2022-08-annual-saas-agreement-fee-cia-live-and-extension-technology-one-class-2.pdf
- **TechnologyOne publishes no price list.** Contracts are negotiated per council.
- **Data-shaped.** Rating is a register (units, values, ratepayers), a calculation (rates set by resolution), a ledger and a handful of statutory steps. No hardware, no network effect.
- **The law is public.** Every rule the system enforces is a section of an Act on legislation.govt.nz.
- **Not covered here:** finance, payroll, procurement, HR, asset management and the customer portal, which are other parts of OneCouncil.

Scores (1 to 5): annual bill 5, data-shaped 5, our usual buyers 1 (councils, not small businesses), search demand 2, one-session buildable 4, import path 3. Total 20 of 30.
