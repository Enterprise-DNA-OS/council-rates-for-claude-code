---
description: Everything about one rating unit: values, ratepayers, this year's rates line by line, ledger and arrears steps.
---

Run `npm run rates -- unit "<assessment number, address or ratepayer name>" --json`. If it lists candidates, show them and ask which. Present: the unit and its values, the ratepayers, the rates lines (rate, basis, factor, amount), the ledger, the balance and the next arrears step. Never guess a unit.
